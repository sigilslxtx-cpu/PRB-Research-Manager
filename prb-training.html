Using this with PRB

Keep one task system: This page describes the training plan. Track actual work, hours, follow-ups and source findings in the existing PRB Research Manager, rather than creating a second research database.

Privacy: This page contains no private family records. Keep living-person information and original records in your private local workspace or access-controlled Drive folders, not on the public site.

Qualifications: Genealogy research, historical investigation and independent appraisal have different competence and legal boundaries. This roadmap does not grant a professional credential or authorize regulated work. Paid launch depends on readiness.

Sunday review: During the FLAME weekly review, check progress on PRB alongside personal, SIGIL and RFR goals. Assign a realistic next week and review your calendar.

Compare credentials and memberships — this week's extra research step

APG is a voluntary professional membership (reported $120 per year in 2026), not a genealogy credential. Benefits. BCG Certified Genealogist (CG) and ICAPGen Accredited Genealogist (AG) are voluntary competency credentials and longer-term goals. BCG uses a portfolio and currently lists $480 across its preliminary and two final application fees, plus $80 annual maintenance after certification; one BCG research-report requirement must be for someone other than your own family. ICAPGen evaluates a regional specialty through a four-generation project, exams and an oral review; verify its current fees. BCG · ICAPGen.

Appraisals: ISA's Core Course lists $1,700 for nonmembers or $1,500 for members and allows six months to complete. A personal property USPAP course is an additional potential cost; verify its exact provider fee. Neither course alone qualifies someone to appraise every kind of object. ISA details.

To record these costs, open Research Manager → Finances → Future expenses & training budget. Review estimates there; they are not counted as paid expenses. No registration is authorized.
